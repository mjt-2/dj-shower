DJ $hower: Stage 0 test

Does the mic keep listening while Spotify keeps playing?

How to test

  1. Start a song in the Spotify app, at normal volume.
  2. Come back to this page in Safari and tap Start mic. Allow mic access.
  3. Watch and listen: does the music pause, get quieter, or sound worse?
  4. Talk or clap. The green bar should jump.
  5. Leave the phone down for 5 minutes, screen on. Check that the bar still moves.
  6. Tap Copy results and paste them to Claude.
